## 2. Current universal formula

The working master expression recovered from the 2026-11-13 discussion is:

\[
\mathbb U(O)=\mathcal T[S,Z,O,\mathbb I,\mathbb R,\mathbb G,\mathbb H]
\]

with the objective-directed transition:

\[
Z_{n+1}=F(Z_n,O,C_n,H_n)
\]

Interpretation for system design:

- \(O\): the objective or direction supplied by the owner/user; it may vary by domain.
- \(Z_n\): the current system state at iteration \(n\).
- \(C_n\): the constraints, evidence, permissions, and operating context available at that iteration.
- \(H_n\): the recorded history/provenance relevant to the transition.
- \(F\): a bounded transition function that proposes the next state; it must not bypass authorization, verification, or safety gates.
- \(\mathcal T\): the transformation/evaluation operator.
- \(S,\mathbb I,\mathbb R,\mathbb G,\mathbb H\): retained framework dimensions whose precise formal definitions remain subject to the user's canonical specification and mathematical validation. They must not be silently redefined by an implementation.

This is an adaptable design equation, not a universal law of nature and not a proven theorem. Any formal claim requires explicit definitions, assumptions, testable propositions, counterexamples, and independent review.

## 3. Universal operating cycle

Every PETS/AEGIS module follows the same cycle:

1. **Orient:** receive objective \(O\), scope, and desired outcome.
2. **Observe:** collect only authorized, relevant inputs.
3. **Classify:** distinguish observed facts, user-supplied claims, inference, hypothesis, symbolic concept, and unknown.
4. **Constrain:** apply safety, privacy, permissions, resource limits, and domain rules.
5. **Reason:** generate candidate transitions and alternatives.
6. **Validate:** test against evidence, invariants, reproducibility, and counterexamples.
7. **Authorize:** require owner approval for external writes, sharing, publication, or consequential actions.
8. **Act:** perform only the approved bounded operation.
9. **Record:** append provenance, hashes when bytes were retrieved, outcomes, errors, and omissions.
10. **Learn:** update non-authoritative working knowledge from validated outcomes; never silently mutate the canonical core.

Compactly:

\[
\text{Objective}\rightarrow\text{Observe}\rightarrow\text{Classify}\rightarrow\text{Constrain}
\rightarrow\text{Reason}\rightarrow\text{Validate}\rightarrow\text{Authorize}
\rightarrow\text{Act}\rightarrow\text{Record}\rightarrow\text{Learn}
\]

## 6. Universal safety and integrity invariants

These invariants apply to every module and connector:

- Local-first where feasible; default-deny for external actions.
- Read-only by default; owner approval for writes.
- No credentials embedded in source, logs, manifests, or model prompts.
- No automatic execution of generated code.
- No autonomous privilege escalation or permission changes.
- No external sharing, invitations, publication, or complaints without explicit scoped authorization.
- Preserve all prior assets; additions are versioned and non-destructive.
- Every collection records source, time, method, revision, scope, result, and limitations.
- Hash only bytes actually retrieved; provider identifiers are not misrepresented as independent hashes.
- Partial inventory remains partial; missing evidence is not proof of nonexistence.
- Symbolic/speculative content stays separate from empirical evidence.
- Pets/companions are presentation interfaces, not autonomous authorities and do not receive credential access or private-knowledge export permissions.
